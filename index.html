How To Play

Select a tower and click an empty part of the map to place it.

Click a tower to inspect and upgrade it.

Towers automatically attack enemies.

Keep enemies away from the red exit.

💧 Water Cannons need water.

🖱️ Scroll to zoom the map.

🖱️ Hold middle mouse and drag to move around.

⌨️ WASD / Arrow Keys move the camera.

⏸ Pause the game or use 2x / 3x speed.

⌨️ E upgrades, X sells, R changes targeting, Q uses abilities, N starts the next wave, K opens achievements.

❄️ Frost Towers slow enemies. 💥 Mortars deal huge splash damage. 📡 Support Towers buff nearby towers.

🎯 Click Target to cycle First, Last, Strong and Close. Upgrades now have three branching paths, each with three tiers.

💰 Banks generate cash automatically and can earn extra money each wave.
